HIIT in simple terms

HIIT means high-intensity interval training. A short hard effort is followed by an easier recovery period, then repeated. It can be a useful way to improve fitness, but it should not ruin the quality of your strength sessions.

For beginners, start gently and build up slowly. Choose a lower-impact option like a bike or fast walking if running or jumping does not feel suitable.
